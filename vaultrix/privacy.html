1. Scope and Application

This Privacy Policy applies specifically to the Vaultrix mobile application and describes the privacy practices associated with the current version of Vaultrix.

Other products, websites, or services operated by PrivoSafe may have separate privacy policies because their functionality and data practices may be different.

2. Who Operates Vaultrix

Vaultrix is a product operated by PrivoSafe. References to "PrivoSafe", "we", "us", or "our" in this policy refer to the operator of Vaultrix.

3. Privacy Architecture

Vaultrix is designed as a local-first, serverless vault application. The core vault experience is designed to work without requiring a PrivoSafe-operated backend to store the contents of your vault.

Your private files, photos, documents, and other vault content are processed and stored locally on your device. PrivoSafe does not receive a routine server-side copy of that private vault content.

Privacy principle: Vaultrix is designed so that the operator does not need access to your private vault content in order for the core vault to function.

4. Information Vaultrix Accesses

Vaultrix may request access to device resources when necessary for features that you explicitly use. Depending on your operating system and the features enabled in the app, this may include:

Resource Why it may be used How Vaultrix handles it
Photos / media Importing or displaying content you choose to place in the vault. Processed for the requested vault operation on the device.
Files / documents Selecting files that you choose to protect in the vault. Used for the requested vault operation.
Biometric authentication Unlocking or protecting the vault when you enable biometric unlock. Authentication is handled by the device operating system.
Device storage Persisting the local encrypted vault and app configuration. Stored locally as required by the application.

Permissions are used only for the functionality for which they are requested. Vaultrix does not intentionally use these permissions to collect unrelated personal information.

5. Information Stored on Your Device

Depending on how you use Vaultrix, the application may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Encrypted files, photos, documents, and other content that you intentionally add to the vault.
  • Vault configuration and application preferences.
  • Security information necessary for the vault to operate.
  • Local metadata required to organize and display your vault.
  • Information required to support authentication features you enable.

The exact information stored locally can vary depending on the features and version of Vaultrix you use.

6. Files, Photos and Documents

Vaultrix allows you to select personal files, photos, documents, and other content for storage in your vault.

This content is intended to remain under your control on your device. Vaultrix does not intentionally use your private vault content for advertising, profiling, data mining, or unrelated product research.

If you export, share, move, or otherwise transfer content using an operating-system feature or another application, that action may cause the selected content to leave Vaultrix. The receiving application or service then handles the information under its own privacy practices.

7. Biometric Authentication

Vaultrix may support biometric authentication such as fingerprint or face authentication when supported by your device.

Vaultrix does not receive your fingerprint image, face image, or biometric template. Biometric matching is performed by the device's operating system or supported secure authentication framework. Vaultrix receives an authentication result needed to continue or deny the requested action.

8. Passwords, Keys and Recovery Information

Your vault may be protected using passwords, keys, recovery information, or other security mechanisms depending on the version and configuration of Vaultrix.

PrivoSafe does not ask you to send your vault password or private encryption keys to us for routine vault operation.

Important: If you lose the credentials or recovery information required to unlock your vault, access to encrypted content may be permanently lost. A privacy architecture that prevents the operator from possessing your private keys can also prevent the operator from recovering your data.

9. Network and Internet Access

The core local vault is designed to operate without requiring a PrivoSafe cloud backend to store your private vault content.

The application or operating system may nevertheless use network connectivity for platform functionality, application distribution, updates, or other features that may be introduced in future versions. Where a feature sends information over a network, the relevant behavior will be described in the applicable documentation or privacy policy update.

Vaultrix does not intentionally transmit your private vault content to PrivoSafe merely because the content exists in your vault.

10. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Vaultrix is designed without advertising as part of its core privacy model. Your private vault content is not used to select or personalize advertisements.

Vaultrix does not intentionally use tracking technologies to build an advertising profile from your private vault content.

If a future version introduces analytics, diagnostics, advertising, or another data-processing technology that materially changes the information handled by Vaultrix, this policy will be updated and the relevant disclosures will be provided.

11. Third-Party Components and Services

Vaultrix may include software libraries or components supplied by third parties. These components can be used to provide functionality such as platform integration, authentication, media handling, encryption, or other application features.

Third-party components are not automatically given access to the contents of your vault. Any data processing performed by a third party is limited by the functionality of the component and the permissions available to it.

If a future third-party service is introduced that collects or processes personal information, this policy and the applicable disclosures will be updated accordingly.

12. Data Sharing and Disclosure

PrivoSafe does not sell or rent your private vault content.

Because Vaultrix is designed as a local-first application, PrivoSafe does not routinely receive your private vault files for storage, analysis, advertising, or profiling.

Information may be disclosed when disclosure is required by applicable law, regulation, court order, or other valid legal process. Nothing in this policy is intended to prevent disclosures that we are legally required to make.

If you intentionally share or export content from Vaultrix to another application, platform, cloud provider, or person, that recipient may process the information independently of Vaultrix.

13. Data Retention

Vault content stored locally by Vaultrix remains on your device until you delete it, remove the vault, clear the application's local data, uninstall the application, or otherwise cause the relevant local storage to be removed.

Because the core vault is designed without a PrivoSafe-operated cloud copy of your private vault content, PrivoSafe cannot remotely retrieve or remotely delete content that exists only on your device.

14. Data Deletion

You can delete content from Vaultrix using the deletion functionality provided by the application. You can also remove local application data using your device's operating-system controls.

Deletion of encrypted vault content may be permanent. Before deleting a vault or clearing application data, make sure you have any recovery information or supported backup you may need.

Since PrivoSafe does not maintain a routine remote copy of your local vault content, there may be no server-side copy for PrivoSafe to delete.

15. Security

We design Vaultrix to protect private information using encryption, device security, and access controls appropriate to the application's architecture.

However, no application, operating system, device, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for protecting your device, authentication credentials, recovery information, and any other secrets used to protect your vault.

Our commitment: We do not intentionally ask you to provide us with your vault password or private encryption keys for routine access to your vault. The purpose of Vaultrix is to keep control of private vault data with the person using the vault.

16. Children's Privacy

Vaultrix is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not intentionally request children's personal information for advertising or profiling.

If you believe that personal information has been provided to PrivoSafe in connection with Vaultrix in a manner that should not have occurred, please contact us using the details below.

17. Your Rights and Choices

You control the private content that you place in your Vaultrix vault. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights relating to personal information processed by a service provider, such as rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing.

For information that exists only locally inside your vault, the primary way to access, modify, export, or delete that information is through Vaultrix and your device.

If you have a privacy request that cannot be handled through the application, contact us using the privacy contact information below.

18. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Vaultrix changes, when new features are introduced, or when legal or platform requirements change.

When we make a material change, we will update the Last updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of Vaultrix after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated policy applies to your use of the application to the extent permitted by applicable law.
