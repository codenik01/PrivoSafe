Clozzy privacy promise

Your relationship data is yours alone. We built Clozzy with a zero-knowledge architecture — we never see your photos, notes, or shared secrets.

  • End-to-end encryption — only you and your partner hold the keys.
  • Zero-knowledge — we cannot access or decrypt your data, ever.
  • No tracking — we don’t monitor your activity or sell your info.
  • Open source — our code is public and regularly audited.
Your intimacy, your privacy. Clozzy is a safe space for two — no data mining, no third-party access, just you and your partner.
